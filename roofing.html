Quick Guide

Repair or replace — how do you know?

✓ Consider Repair When

  • •Roof is under 15 years old
  • •Damage is limited to one area
  • •Structure is sound overall
  • •Repair cost is under 30% of replacement

→ Consider Replacement When

  • •Roof is over 20 years old
  • •Damage covers more than 30% of surface
  • •Multiple leaks in different areas
  • •Repairs are becoming more frequent

Not sure which applies? A professional inspection is the clearest way to find out. We'll give you an honest assessment — no pressure, no obligation.
